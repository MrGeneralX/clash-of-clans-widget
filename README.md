# Clash Timers — Android Home Screen Widget

An offline, battery-friendly Android home screen widget that tracks your live **Clash of Clans** Builder, Laboratory, Pet House, and Builder Base upgrade timers directly on your home screen.

## Features
- **Live Home Screen Countdowns:** Uses native Android chronometers and green progress bars to tick down every active upgrade in real time without draining your battery.
- **One-Tap Clipboard Sync:** Copy your Village Data JSON in-game, swipe home, and tap **↻ Sync** on the widget.
- **One-Tap Boost Buttons:** Tap **+1** (Builder, Research, or Pet Potion) or **Bite** directly on the widget to update remaining times instantly without re-syncing.
- **Completion Alerts:** Sends a notification the moment a builder, lab research, or pet upgrade finishes.
- **100% Offline & Private:** Requires zero internet permissions and never asks for your account login.

## How to Download & Install
1. Open the https://github.com/MrGeneralX/clash-of-clans-widget/releases/latest page.
2. Under **Assets**, tap the `.apk` file to download it to your Android phone.
3. Open the downloaded `.apk` and tap **Install** *(allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser if prompted)*.
4. Open **Clash Timers** once to grant notification permissions and set your village display name.
5. Long-press an empty space on your home screen → **Widgets** → drag **Clash Timers** onto your screen and resize it.

## How to Sync Your Village
1. Open **Clash of Clans**.
2. Go to **Settings → More Settings → JSON Data Export → Copy**.
3. Go back to your home screen and tap **↻ Sync** on the widget.
4. You will have to copy JSON everytime if you start new building, lab or pet upgrade.
